Know your environment

Why bushfire awareness matters

South Australia can experience dangerous bushfire weather during hot, dry and windy conditions. The Adelaide Hills and other areas with dry vegetation, steep terrain or homes close to bushland can face particular challenges.

  • ✓Weather can change quickly. Heat, low humidity and strong winds can increase fire danger.
  • ✓Every location is different. Check local risk information and know more than one route away from danger.
  • ✓Preparation takes time. Make decisions early, not when a fire is close.
Remember: This page does not provide live risk ratings. Use the CFS and Bureau of Meteorology for current warnings, fire danger ratings and weather information.

Practical guidance

Prepare. Respond. Recover.

Use these starting points to learn what you can do before, during and after a bushfire. Always follow current official advice for your situation.

Prepare

Make a household bushfire plan, prepare an emergency kit, keep important documents accessible and discuss what you will do if conditions worsen.

Build your checklist →

Respond

Monitor official warnings, act on your plan early and follow directions from emergency services. Do not rely on social media rumours.

Check official warnings →

Recover

After a fire, avoid damaged areas, watch for hazards and use official recovery services for information about assistance and returning home.

Interactive planning aid

Your preparedness starter checklist

Tick off these general preparation tasks as a starting point. This checklist does not replace the detailed guidance or plan recommended by the CFS.

1

Discuss your plan

Agree on where household members will go, how you will contact each other and what you will do if separated.

2

Prepare essentials

Think about water, medications, a first-aid kit, phone chargers, important documents, pet needs and supplies for several days.

3

Know where to check

Bookmark official warnings, know your local area and identify routes and alternatives before fire danger increases.
